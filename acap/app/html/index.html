Thresholds
Confidence55%
Detections below this are discarded. Scores land on steps of about 4 points — the score tensor is 8-bit.
IoU45%
How much two boxes may overlap before the weaker one is suppressed.
Max area85%
Drops boxes covering more of the frame than this. On close scenes the model emits a stable frame-filling box that overlap suppression cannot reach. 100 turns the filter off.
